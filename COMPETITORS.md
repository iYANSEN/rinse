## What we took, and why
- **Ghost-overlay "after" camera** (CompanyCam's signature, now a commodity): the single biggest time saver, because same-angle shots pair themselves. Built in, works offline.
- **Hosted gallery link with branding** (CompanyCam Pro-tier feature): logo, colour, phone, review button; stable link on update; `og:image` so it previews in a text message.
- **Review request** (NiceJob / CompanyCam Marketing add-on): one button on the client page.
- **Share-ready image per pair** for Facebook/Instagram; pressure washing is sold on visuals.
- **CRM hook that carries the gallery URL**, so the link lands in the customer record.

## What we deliberately did not do
- **No AI-generated "after" photos.** Competitors do; clients and insurers need real work. AI here only sorts, pairs and writes captions.
- No team maps, checklists, payments or scheduling: that's where per-seat pricing comes from.

## Positioning to test with the owner
Flat price, unlimited crew members, unlimited jobs. Price is a business decision; the research only shows the seat-based competition starts near $79/month before extra users.
